WH Mocap Viewer

King throw animations — Tekken 3 vs Tekken 7, skeleton visualization of the real clip data (not game renders). Thrower cyan, victim orange.
T3
T7
Data: FK'd world joint positions from the real source clips (T3 king-pack rows, T7 wh-anim/1 clips), normalized into one pair frame — thrower base (0,0,0) facing +X, victim base (+1.0,0,0) facing −X. Camera: fixed orthographic 3/4 view (azimuth 32°, elevation 14°), per-frame follow center, fixed scale per clip pair. Ended clips hold their last frame (HELD). T7 victim clips include a get-up tail — the recovery at the end is not part of the throw. See README.md for the clip format.
